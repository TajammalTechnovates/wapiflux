## 📌 What is WapiFlux? — What does this project do?

WapiFlux is a WhatsApp automation platform built on Meta's official WhatsApp Cloud API — not unofficial bots or browser sessions. Businesses connect their WhatsApp Business account, then run campaigns, automate conversations with chatbots and visual flows, manage customers in a shared team inbox and track results with live analytics — all in one workspace.

## 🎯 Who is it for?

Businesses, shops, teams and agencies that sell and support customers on WhatsApp and want safe, official automation instead of risky unofficial bots.

## ⭐ Key Features & Services — What it offers

- ✅ Official WhatsApp Cloud API — secure, compliant, no unofficial bots
- ✅ Embedded signup to connect WhatsApp Business in minutes
- ✅ Shared team inbox with AI & team agents and chat routing
- ✅ AI chatbot + chatbot replies to answer customers 24/7
- ✅ Visual Flow Maker for automated customer journeys
- ✅ Message templates management and QR codes to start conversations
- ✅ WhatsApp campaigns to opted-in customers with live analytics & chat reports
- ✅ API & webhook access (Pro / Business plans) — plans $3–$20/month, free trial available
